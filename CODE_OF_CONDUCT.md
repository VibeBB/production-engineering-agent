# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.
Participants must be considerate, use welcoming language, accept
professional feedback, and focus critique on technical work rather than
people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and
publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently exclude participants for conduct that violates this policy.
Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. This policy is
adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
